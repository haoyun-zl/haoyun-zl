**Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Project Lead**

#### Contributions

- Led the project concept, research framework design, and team coordination.
- Took the lead in reviewing **50+ Chinese and English papers**, translating part of the core content, and comparing research methods on talent mobility.

#### Output

- Approved as a **Shanghai-level Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program** project.

### Market-oriented Reform and ESG Performance: Evidence from Registration-based IPO Reform

**Independent Author**

An empirical study on how market-oriented reform affected corporate **ESG** performance in the context of registration-based IPO reform.

#### Contributions

- Selected listed firms in the registration-based IPO pilot as the treatment group and matched them with a control group using **PSM**.
- Self-studied econometrics and statistical software, then conducted multi-period **DID** empirical analysis in **Stata**.
- Explored how ESG performance responded to new listing policies under different scenarios.
- Independently reviewed nearly one hundred Chinese and English papers and advanced the research design and paper drafting.

#### Output

- Completed the **first draft of the paper**.

`ESG` · `IPO Reform` · `PSM` · `DID` · `Stata`
